# US4 Validation - Coffee Panel Toggle

## Steps
1. Open landing page and locate Coffee control in category navigation.
2. Activate Coffee control.
3. Confirm Coffee options panel becomes visible.
4. Activate Coffee control again.
5. Confirm Coffee options panel is hidden.
6. Open panel once more and use Close button.
7. Confirm panel closes and focus remains usable in navigation controls.

## Result
- Coffee control toggles panel visibility reliably.
- Panel can be closed both by re-activating trigger and by Close button.
- No UI instability observed during repeated open/close interactions.
