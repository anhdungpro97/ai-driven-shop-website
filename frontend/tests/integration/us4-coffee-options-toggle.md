# US4 Validation - Coffee Full-Screen Toggle

## Steps
1. Open landing page and locate Coffee control in category navigation.
2. Activate Coffee control.
3. Confirm Coffee options full-screen view becomes visible.
4. Activate Coffee control again.
5. Confirm Coffee options full-screen view is hidden.
6. Open view once more and use Close button.
7. Confirm view closes and focus remains usable in navigation controls.
8. Re-open and press Escape to close.

## Result
- Coffee control toggles full-screen view visibility reliably.
- View can be closed by re-activating trigger, Close button, and Escape key.
- No UI instability observed during repeated open/close interactions.
