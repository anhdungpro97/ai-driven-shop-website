# US6 Validation - Responsive and Accessibility Readability

## Steps
1. Open Coffee options full-screen view on desktop viewport.
2. Verify view contrast, spacing, and typography align with existing landing design.
3. Resize to mobile viewport and reopen view.
4. Verify no overlap or clipping in item rows and action controls.
5. Use keyboard: tab to Coffee trigger, open view, and close with Escape.

## Result
- Coffee full-screen view remains readable and visually consistent on desktop and mobile.
- Name/image/price/action rows remain legible without clipping on small screens.
- Keyboard interaction supports opening and closing view behavior.
