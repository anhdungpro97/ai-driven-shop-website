# US6 Validation - Responsive and Accessibility Readability

## Steps
1. Open Coffee options panel on desktop viewport.
2. Verify panel contrast, spacing, and typography align with existing landing design.
3. Resize to mobile viewport and reopen panel.
4. Verify no overlap or clipping in panel rows.
5. Use keyboard: tab to Coffee trigger, open panel, and close panel with Escape.

## Result
- Coffee panel remains readable and visually consistent on desktop and mobile.
- Name/price rows remain legible without clipping on small screens.
- Keyboard interaction supports opening and closing panel behavior.
